The evidence packet contains deterministic horizon rows. Keep these measures separate:

- `next_game_score` is the next-game position-relative percentile. It may include a schedule-aware historical matchup factor, but that factor is descriptive context and not a complete defensive projection. Current availability must remain visible.
- `rest_of_season_score` is the rest-of-season position-relative percentile based on the production baseline and scheduled games when coverage is complete. It is not recovery-adjusted when no recovery timeline is modeled.
- A matchup factor changes next-game points only when `next_game_matchup_adjustment_status=applied`; limited, unavailable, or non-improving holdout evidence is descriptive only.
- `dynasty_score` is the external market plus timeline lens. It is not a career-points forecast.
- `career_projection_score` is an internal five-year age-curve scenario. When `career_history_status=matched`, it is anchored to the current projection plus recency-weighted historical nflverse production through the reported season; preserve the reported `career_history_source_player_id` and join method as the identity receipt. When history is unavailable or ambiguous, the basis must say so. It is not a lifetime forecast or a guarantee.
- `contender_fit_score` and `rebuilder_fit_score` are weighted combinations of the separate clocks. Explain the weighting receipt when useful, but do not replace the component scores with one universal grade.
- `rest_of_season_minus_next_game_delta`, `dynasty_minus_rest_of_season_delta`, and `career_minus_dynasty_delta` are later-clock minus earlier-clock percentile transitions. Use them to explain a short-term or long-term premium; do not call them prices or additional scores. If a delta is unavailable, do not infer it from a missing endpoint.
- `fit_coverage` reports how many of the four clocks are available. If fit coverage is partial, say which clock is missing when it materially limits the contender/rebuilder comparison; the fit weights are renormalized across the available components.

- `current_availability_status=no_current_nfl_team` means Sleeper currently has no NFL team for the player. Historical production can inform a conditional dynasty/signing case, but next-game and rest-of-season scores should remain unavailable, with low confidence, until a current team and role exist.
- `market_value` is the cross-position price anchor. The horizon scores and all fit scores are position-relative percentiles from 0-100, so do not rank a QB's 80th percentile against a WR's 70th percentile as though that were a trade-price ranking.
- The four `*_minus_market_delta` fields compare each available clock with the player's same-position market percentile. Use a positive or negative delta to identify a repricing lead and name the window; it is not a dollar gap or proof of mispricing. If the clock is missing, do not infer its delta.
